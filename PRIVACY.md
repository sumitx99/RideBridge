# Privacy Policy — RideBridge

**Effective date:** 18 October 2026

RideBridge is a web app that lets you pick an exact destination on a map and open a ride app (Uber or Rapido) with that destination filled in. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

## Summary

- RideBridge has **no user accounts, no database and no server** of its own.
- We **do not collect, store or sell** your personal information.
- To work, the app sends some information to **Google Maps Platform**, and passes your chosen destination to **Uber** or **Rapido** when you tap a booking button.

## Information the app uses

### Your location
If you allow it, your browser shares your current location so the map can start near you. This location is used only inside your browser to centre the map. RideBridge does not send it to us or store it.

### Searches and selected places
When you search for a place, browse nearby categories, or tap a place on the map, the app sends your search text and map position to **Google Maps Platform** (Maps JavaScript API and Places API) to show results, place details and photos. Google processes this information under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Booking a ride
When you tap **Book with Uber** or **Book with Rapido**, the app opens that service with the destination's coordinates, name and address. From that point, the ride service's own privacy policy applies:

- [Uber Privacy Notice](https://www.uber.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Rapido Privacy Policy](https://www.rapido.bike/privacy)

When a ride app is not available on your device, the app may copy the destination coordinates to your clipboard instead.

## What we do not do

- We do not create accounts or ask for your name, phone number or email.
- We do not use our own cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
- We do not store your search history, locations or bookings.
- We do not share or sell any information to third parties.

## Third-party services

RideBridge relies on Google Maps Platform, Uber and Rapido. These services may collect information according to their own policies, which we do not control.

## Children

RideBridge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
